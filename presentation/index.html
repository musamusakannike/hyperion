The Problem

Daily Campus Transportation Challenges

Thousands of students board campus shuttle buses daily, facing repetitive payment bottlenecks.

Cash & Change Scarcity

Looking for exact ₦250 cash or bus change creates long delays, disputes, and boarding friction every morning.

Slow Boarding Queues

Manual cash collection by drivers or conductors slows passenger flow, leading to congestion at the campus bus parks.

Zero Trip Transparency

Neither students nor transit administrators have an easy way to verify transactions, monitor rides, or track spending.

The Solution

Hyperion: Integrated Cashless Ecosystem

A synchronized system connecting physical bus terminals, mobile students, and cloud records.

1

Smart Ride Points

Students purchase ride points (1 Point = ₦250 ride). The wallet stays safely linked to their student profile.

2

Instant Tap or Scan

Board the bus in under a second using an RFID student card or by scanning the bus QR code with a smartphone.

3

Automated Verification

The on-board validator verifies the fare, beeps green, shows balance on the LCD screen, and logs the trip immediately.

Hardware Implementation

The Hyperion Bus Validator Pod

Physical terminal mounted at the campus bus entrance for hands-free driver operations.

Finished Physical Hardware Unit
  • Contactless RFID Card Zone: Students tap their ID card or fob for instant payment without opening a phone.
  • 16×2 Backlit LCD Screen: Displays live passenger feedback: ride deduction and remaining points.
  • Dual LED & Buzzer Audio Feedback: Green double-beep for approved boarding; red alert for low balance.
  • Permanent Boarding QR Code: Alternative digital boarding option for students using the mobile web app.

System Operation

Seamless Tap or Scan Boarding

Designed for high reliability whether a student has a smartphone or not.

Method 1: Tap RFID Card

  • Student touches card on the validator pod.
  • Validator reads card UID and checks cloud balance.
  • Green light flashes, buzzer sounds, screen confirms.

Method 2: Scan Bus QR

  • Student opens Hyperion app on their smartphone.
  • Points phone camera at the bus boarding QR code.
  • 1 Point deducted; trip appears instantly in history.
Software Experience

Unified Software Ecosystem

Dedicated operational interfaces built for students, bus drivers, and campus transit administrators.

Student Portal
Mobile / Web
  • Point Balance: Instant display of remaining ride passes without cash calculations.
  • Quick Actions: One-tap QR scanning for rapid bus boarding & instant online top-up.
  • Trip Log: Real-time transparent history of ride timestamps and points deducted.
Driver Terminal
In-Vehicle Hub
  • Tap Validation: Instant green/red visual response for card taps and QR boarding.
  • Capacity Monitor: Live onboard headcount counter to prevent bus overloading.
  • Shift Ledger: Live tracking of completed routes, passenger counts, and shift fare stats.
Admin Hub
Central Control
  • Fleet Telemetry: Live campus map tracking active buses, queues, and route velocity.
  • Financial Audit: Automated revenue reporting, top-up logs, and driver payout reconciliation.
  • Card Management: Instant NFC card issuance, blocking lost cards, and user role access.
