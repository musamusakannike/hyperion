THE SOLUTION

INTEGRATED CASHLESS ECOSYSTEM

A synchronized 3-step transit network connecting cards, bus hardware, and cloud records.

01
PHASE 01

Smart Ride Wallet

Students purchase digital ride passes in advance (1 Point = ₦250). The balance remains securely tied to their student ID profile.

1 PASS = ₦250 FLAT FARE
02
PHASE 02

Sub-Second Boarding

Board the shuttle under one second by tapping a contactless RFID card or scanning the bus QR code with a smartphone.

< 1.0s RAPID PASSENGER ENTRY
03
PHASE 03

Automated Validation

The on-board validator verifies fare, beeps green, shows balance on LCD, and logs the trip immediately to the central cloud.

SOFTWARE PLATFORM

UNIFIED SOFTWARE ECOSYSTEM

Dedicated operational interfaces built for students, bus drivers, and transit administrators.

Student Portal Mobile / Web
  • Point Balance: Instant pass count without cash calculations.
  • Quick QR Scan: Rapid camera boarding & online top-up.
  • Trip Log: Real-time history of ride timestamps and deductions.
Driver Terminal In-Vehicle Hub
  • Tap Validation: Visual green/red response for card scans.
  • Capacity Monitor: Live onboard counter to prevent overloading.
  • Shift Ledger: Real-time route passenger and fare totals.
Admin Hub Central Control
  • Fleet Telemetry: Campus map tracking active shuttle buses.
  • Financial Audit: Automated revenue and driver payout records.
  • Card Management: Instant NFC card issuance and blocking.
