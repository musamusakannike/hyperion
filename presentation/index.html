Next-gen cashless transit for a moving campus

HYPERION replaces cash, queues, and guesswork on campus shuttles with instant tap-and-go validation — powered by custom IoT hardware and a live fleet network.

Sub-300ms validation
100% cashless
The problem

Campus transit is still stuck in cash

Three frictions compound daily for students, drivers, and administrators alike.

Cash friction & change scarcity

Drivers lose minutes per boarding hunting for change, and students are routinely turned away for lacking exact fare.

Zero transit visibility

No record of ridership, revenue, or route performance exists — fare leakage goes undetected and unaddressed.

Long boarding queues

Manual fare collection bottlenecks every stop, delaying shuttles and stacking up frustrated students.

The solution

One system, three connected pillars

Pillar 01

Student mobile & web app

Top up a dedicated account, hold a live point balance, and tap through boarding with a QR code or RFID card.

Pillar 02

ESP32 validator pod

Onboard hardware reads RFID or QR in under 300ms, confirms fare deduction, and gives instant visual/audio feedback.

Pillar 03

Admin command portal

Live dashboards for ridership, revenue reconciliation, fleet health, and RFID card provisioning.

Student experience

From top-up to boarding, in four steps

STEP 01

Dedicated NUBAN

Every student gets a personal virtual bank account on signup.

STEP 02

Instant point top-up

Bank transfer converts instantly — ₦250 = 1 point.

STEP 03

QR scan or RFID tap

Board and present the app's QR code or a tapped RFID card.

STEP 04

Sub-second clearance

Fare deducts and the gate clears in under a second.

IoT hardware

The Validator Pod

A purpose-built onboard unit that reads, confirms, and clears fare — with no dependency on a live connection.

  • ESP32 microcontroller running the validation firmware
  • PN532 / RC522 RFID reader for card taps
  • 0.96" OLED screen for balance and status
  • Dual LEDs + piezo buzzer for instant pass/fail feedback
  • Servo gate lock simulator for access control
<300ms latency Offline resilient
Fintech & point economy

Money in, points out — automatically

Automated dedicated NUBANs

Paystack provisions a unique virtual account per student at signup — no manual onboarding step.

Real-time webhook reconciliation

Incoming transfers trigger a webhook that credits points to the student's balance within seconds.

Booth top-up fail-safe

Physical top-up booths cover students without banking access, keeping the system fully inclusive.

₦250 = 1 pt

A simple, transparent conversion rate students can track at a glance.
