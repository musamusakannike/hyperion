The Problem

Three critical friction points compound daily for students, drivers, and park administrators alike.

Cash Friction & Change Scarcity

Drivers lose minutes per boarding hunting for change notes, and students are routinely turned away or delayed for lacking exact denominations.

Bottleneck: High Fare Delay

Zero Transit Visibility

Bottleneck: Revenue Leakage

Security & Isolation Architecture

  • JWT Session Authentication: Cryptographically verified tokens for mobile, web, and driver dispatch sessions.
  • Bcrypt Hashing: Salted password and PIN encryption protecting user credentials at rest.
  • Pod Device Key Isolation: Every ESP32 pod authenticates with dedicated API keys to prevent device spoofing.
