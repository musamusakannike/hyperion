02
The Campus Transit Crisis
PROBLEM & VALUE PROPOSITION
EXISTING BOTTLENECK
The Friction of Cash on Campus

Over 30,000 university students rely on campus shuttle buses daily. Physical cash payments cause severe cash-change shortages, delayed boarding queues, fare dispute friction, and untracked driver revenue leakages.

The Hyperion Solution: A unified digital wallet where students can buy Ride Points (e.g. 1 Ride Point = ₦250). Students authenticate via physical student ID card or dynamic driver QR code for instantaneous, cashless boarding.

12 MIN
Queue Delay Eliminated

Sub-second tap-and-go boarding accelerates passenger entry by over 80% compared to cash handling.

100%
Audited Revenue Reconciliation

All transactions sync to university administrative consoles with zero physical cash leakage.

Full-Stack Architecture
HARDWARE-SOFTWARE CO-DESIGN
LAYER 01 CLIENT APP
Student Mobile App

The daily passenger app for students to quickly generate QR boarding passes, check live bus seat availability, view wallet balances, and top up funds.

TECH: REACT NATIVE · EXPO · TAILWIND
LAYER 02 EDGE IoT
Smart Bus Scanner Pod

The physical scanner mounted inside the bus. It instantly reads student ID cards or phone taps, shows instant approval on its screen, and signals boarding access.

TECH: C++ · ESP-IDF · FREERTOS · PN532
LAYER 03 BACKEND API
Central Cloud Server

The secure brain connecting everything. It instantly verifies rides, processes fare deductions, and syncs data between mobile apps and bus scanners in milliseconds.

TECH: NODE.JS · TYPESCRIPT · MONGOOSE
LAYER 04 FINTECH & FLEET
Payments & Admin Hub

Provides each student with their own dedicated bank account for easy bank transfers, while giving managers real-time bus tracking and revenue insights.

TECH: PAYSTACK WEBHOOKS · MONGODB ATLAS

Offline Reliability

Network Outage Detection

If the bus drives into an area with poor network coverage or dead zones, the scanner automatically switches to offline mode without interrupting boarding.

AUTOMATIC SWITCH

Internal Memory Verification

The scanner stores an encrypted list of valid student passes directly on its internal memory, verifying card taps in less than 50 milliseconds.

SECURE LOGGING
